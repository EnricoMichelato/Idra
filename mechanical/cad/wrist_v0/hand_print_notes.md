# Hand v2.6: printing and assembly notes

v2.6 is 13.5 mm shorter with the same motor and grip (108 mm from the bottom to the top of the dome): the roll-shaft hub is mounted upside down with its neck sunk in the bottom plate, the worm thread is cut to 16 mm, and the washer under the worm hub is gone because the thrust goes up.

v2.5 changes the finger supports and the palm. The three worm wheels now turn in radial slots of one **turret** on the head: three hollow pillars whose flanks are 9 mm cheeks, instead of six separate 5 mm cheeks. The palm is a **dome** dropped on from above and screwed into the cheek tops, right beside the wheels; three plugs under it fit into the pillars and stop the finger pins. The worm thrust goes dome → turret → body, without long legs.

v2.4 is the strong-grip hand: a 25 mm metal gearmotor, a bought steel worm held at both ends, and a metal hub on the roll shaft. About 16 N per finger; the numbers are in `control/hand_grip_report.md`. The hand is 46 mm longer than v2.3.

From v2.3 the hand is passive: no ESP32 and no power module, only a small board with a rectifier, a DRV8871 and two filters. Schematic and bench test are in `electronics/hand_passive.md`.

STLs are in `out/print/` (generated by `python3 export_print.py`), already oriented for printing, with the lowest point at z = 0. The `_xN` suffix tells how many copies to print.

| `palm_PETG` | PETG | 1 | flat top on the bed, plugs up | no | palm dome: pocket for the F6-12M thrust bearing, 45° flank, three plugs that fit the pillar pockets |

**Recommended settings**
- **Structural PETG** (roots, head, body): 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top and bottom layers, 40% gyroid.
- **Wheels (finger roots):** module 1 teeth: 0.4 mm nozzle or smaller, 6 perimeters, 100% infill around the teeth. PA-CF is better than PETG here.
- **TPU 95A:** 0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 10–15% gyroid, low speed. The Fin Ray walls (2.8 mm) and ribs (2.4 mm) come out as solid perimeters.
- **Elephant foot:** turn on compensation in the slicer, because some parts have mating faces on the bed (head groove, wheels).

**Why these orientations**
- **Finger roots:** printed flat, the layers lie in the plane in which the finger bends, so bending does not open the layers. The wheel teeth also print flat, like a normal gear.
- **Head:** with the plate on the bed, the turret walls grow vertically; the only bridges are the Ø5 pin holes.
- **Palm dome:** upside down, its 45° flank and the plugs print without supports.
- **Vents:** they have a 45° pointed roof, so they print without bridges.

## Clearances already in the model
- **Pins in the wheels:** Ø5.2 hole in the wheels (they turn), Ø5.0 in the cheeks (ream with a 5 mm drill). The pin goes in from outside, through a hole in the pillar wall.
- **Dome plugs:** 0.3 mm to the pocket walls, 0.4 mm to each pin end.
- **Fin Ray on the tenon:** 14 × 10 mm PETG tenon, socket in the TPU +0.2 mm per side.
- **Lip and groove:** body lip 1.2 × 1.8 mm, head groove +0.2 mm.
- **Board rails:** +0.25 mm per side.

## Small hardware
- **Finger pins:** 3 hardened dowel pins Ø5 × 32 mm. No clips: the dome plugs hold them.
- **Fin Ray:** 6 × M3 × 25 screws + 6 nuts + 12 large washers (2 per finger, washers on the TPU).
- **Head on body:** 3 × M3 × 8 on the floor of the pillar pockets, into 3 M3 heat-set inserts in the body.
- **Dome on head:** 6 × M3 × 10 into 6 M3 heat-set inserts in the cheek tops, one on each side of every wheel.
- **Gear motor:** 25 mm metal gearmotor, 12 V, 75:1; 2 × M3 × 6 screws 17 mm apart: check them on the motor you buy.
- **Limit switch:** 12.8 × 5.8 × 6.5 micro switch (KW10/D2F type) + 2 × M2 × 10 screws.
- **Worm:** steel, module 1, pitch Ø16, single start, **right hand**, with hub and M4 set screw (KHK SW1-R1 class). **Cut the thread to 16 mm** from the free end (cut-off wheel, then deburr): the three wheels use a 13 mm band. Its bore is 6 mm: use a 4/6 mm sleeve with a cross hole on the motor shaft, or buy one with a 4 mm bore.
- **Worm top:** Ø6 × 16 dowel pin with Loctite in the worm bore, F6-12M thrust ball bearing in the palm dome. The grip load pushes the worm up, into this bearing.
- **Roll shaft hub:** aluminium flange hub, 12 mm bore, mounted **neck down** in a pocket of the bottom; 4 × M3 × 8 into inserts in the hand bottom; set screws on the shaft flat, reached through two Ø3.2 holes in the side of the bottom (0° and 270°).

## Assembly order
1. M3 heat-set inserts: three in the bosses of the body, four in the bottom for the hub, six in the cheek tops of the head.
2. RX coil stack into the pocket under the bottom (2.4 mm deep): two rings of 0.3 mm ferrite sheet, then the PCB coil `idra_coil_RX24`, copper face outwards and flush. Solder the leads to the back of the pads first and pass them through the two Ø2.6 holes under the pads.
3. Flange hub into the bottom, neck down, set screws lined up with the two side holes, 4 × M3 × 8. Push the roll shaft up through the bottom into the hub and tighten the set screws on the flat through the side holes.
4. Slide the driver board (20 × 25 mm) into its rail from above and put the 47 µF capacitor in its seat. Wire the limit switch on its NC contact, in series with IN2.
5. On the head: limit switch with 2 × M2 (wires through the hole next to it), gear motor screwed from above with 2 × M3. Then the worm on the shaft (sleeve first, 0.3 mm above the plate), set screw on the D flat (the key goes through the hole in the pillar at 180°).
6. Head on body: the lip goes into the groove. Fit the 3 × M3 × 8 through the open pillars.
7. Fin Ray parts onto the roots, 2 × M3 per finger. Fingers into the turret slots, all at the same angle (fingers parallel, 0°). The height offset of the three wheels is already in the model, so they mesh in phase. Push each pin in from outside until it is centred (about 11 mm below the wall surface).
8. Dowel pin with Loctite into the top of the worm. Thrust bearing into the dome, dome over the pin with its plugs in the pillars, then the 6 × M3 × 10.
9. First start: open until the limit switch clicks (fingers at +55/+60°). That is the zero. From fully open to tips touching (−9°) takes about 5.4 turns of the worm, a little over 3 s.

## Travel
- **Finger angle:** measured from vertical, 0° = fingers parallel.
- **Closed:** −9°, tips touching. It can squeeze a little more because the TPU gives.
- **80 mm apple grasp:** +26°.
- **Fully open:** +60°. The limit switch triggers over the last ~4.7°.
