# Hand v2.4: printing and assembly notes

v2.4 is the strong-grip hand: a 25 mm metal gearmotor, a bought steel worm held at both ends, and a metal hub on the roll shaft. About 16 N per finger; the numbers are in `control/hand_grip_report.md`. The hand is 46 mm longer than v2.3.

From v2.3 the hand is passive: no ESP32 and no power module, only a small board with a rectifier, a DRV8871 and two filters. Schematic and bench test are in `electronics/hand_passive.md`.

STLs are in `out/print/` (generated by `python3 export_print.py`), already oriented for printing, with the lowest point at z = 0. The `_xN` suffix tells how many copies to print.

## Printed parts

| Part | Material | Qty | Orientation | Supports | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hand_body_PETG` | PETG | 1 | upright, skirt on the bed | only inside the skirt (Ø65, under the bottom) | M3 inserts in the 3 bosses; rail for the driver board (at 0°) |
| `hand_head_PETG` | PETG | 1 | plate on the bed, cheeks up | no | R10 fillets at the base of the cheeks; groove underneath for the body lip |
| `finger_root_with_lobe_PETG` | PETG | 1 | flat (wheel axis vertical) | no | finger 1, with the limit switch cam |
| `finger_root_PETG` | PETG | 2 | flat | no | fingers 2 and 3 |
| `finray_TPU` | TPU 95A | 3 | flat (18 mm thickness along Z) | no | slides onto the root tenon |
| `palm_PETG` | PETG | 1 | top face on the bed, legs up | no | palm cap: pocket for the F6-12M thrust bearing, 3 legs on the head screws |

**Recommended settings**
- **Structural PETG** (roots, head, body): 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top and bottom layers, 40% gyroid.
- **Wheels (finger roots):** module 1 teeth: 0.4 mm nozzle or smaller, 6 perimeters, 100% infill around the teeth. PA-CF is better than PETG here.
- **TPU 95A:** 0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 10–15% gyroid, low speed. The Fin Ray walls (2.8 mm) and ribs (2.4 mm) come out as solid perimeters.
- **Elephant foot:** turn on compensation in the slicer, because some parts have mating faces on the bed (head groove, wheels).

**Why these orientations**
- **Finger roots:** printed flat, the layers lie in the plane in which the finger bends, so bending does not open the layers. The wheel teeth also print flat, like a normal gear.
- **Head:** with the plate on the bed, the cheeks grow vertically and the R10 fillets have no overhangs.
- **Vents:** they have a 45° pointed roof, so they print without bridges.

## Clearances already in the model
- **Pins in the wheels:** Ø5.2 hole in the wheels (they turn), Ø5.0 in the cheeks (pin locked; ream with a 5 mm drill).
- **Fin Ray on the tenon:** 14 × 10 mm PETG tenon, socket in the TPU +0.2 mm per side.
- **Lip and groove:** body lip 1.2 × 1.8 mm, head groove +0.2 mm.
- **Board rails:** +0.25 mm per side.

## Small hardware
- **Finger pins:** 3 steel pins Ø5 × 24 mm with 6 DIN 6799 e-clips (or 3 M5 × 25 screws with lock nuts).
- **Fin Ray:** 6 × M3 × 25 screws + 6 nuts + 12 large washers (2 per finger, washers on the TPU).
- **Palm and head on body:** 3 × M3 × 45 through the palm legs and the head into 3 M3 heat-set inserts in the body.
- **Gear motor:** 25 mm metal gearmotor, 12 V, 75:1; 2 × M3 × 6 screws 17 mm apart: check them on the motor you buy.
- **Limit switch:** 12.8 × 5.8 × 6.5 micro switch (KW10/D2F type) + 2 × M2 × 10 screws.
- **Worm:** steel, module 1, pitch Ø16, single start, **right hand**, with hub and M4 set screw (KHK SW1-R1 class). Its bore is 6 mm: use a 4/6 mm sleeve with a cross hole on the motor shaft, or buy one with a 4 mm bore.
- **Worm top:** Ø6 × 16 dowel pin with Loctite in the worm bore, F6-12M thrust ball bearing in the palm cap. The grip load pushes the worm up, into this bearing.
- **Roll shaft hub:** aluminium flange hub, 12 mm bore, 4 × M3 × 8 into inserts in the hand bottom, set screws on the shaft flat.
- **Thrust washer:** 1 washer 10 × 4.4 × 1, brass or PTFE, under the worm hub.

## Assembly order
1. M3 heat-set inserts: three in the bosses of the body, four in the bottom for the hub.
2. RX coil into its ferrite cup (Ø44/15, 1.5 mm bottom, rims flush with the coil), all in the seat under the bottom (3.5 mm deep). The leads go through a hole in the bottom of the ferrite, then through the Ø3.2 hole.
3. Flange hub onto the bottom with 4 × M3 × 8. Push the roll shaft up through the bottom into the hub and tighten the set screws on the flat.
4. Slide the driver board (20 × 25 mm) into its rail from above and put the 47 µF capacitor in its seat. Wire the limit switch on its NC contact, in series with IN2.
5. On the head: limit switch with 2 × M2 (wires through the hole next to it), gear motor screwed from above with 2 × M3. Then the thrust washer and the worm on the shaft (sleeve first), set screw on the D flat.
6. Head on body: the lip goes into the groove. Do not fit the screws yet.
7. Fin Ray parts onto the roots, 2 × M3 per finger. Fingers between the cheeks, all at the same angle (fingers parallel, 0°). The height offset of the three wheels is already in the model, so they mesh in phase. Insert the pins and e-clips.
8. Dowel pin with Loctite into the top of the worm. Thrust bearing into the palm cap, palm cap over the pin, then the 3 × M3 × 45 through the legs and the head into the body.
9. First start: open until the limit switch clicks (fingers at +55/+60°). That is the zero. From fully open to tips touching (−9°) takes about 5.4 turns of the worm, a little over 3 s.

## Travel
- **Finger angle:** measured from vertical, 0° = fingers parallel.
- **Closed:** −9°, tips touching. It can squeeze a little more because the TPU gives.
- **80 mm apple grasp:** +26°.
- **Fully open:** +60°. The limit switch triggers over the last ~4.7°.
