# PCB coils for the wrist link

Three ring boards generated by `electronics/coil_pcb.py`. Each one replaces a hand-wound Litz coil in the Ø44 / Ø15 pocket of the wrist flange (TX) or of the hand bottom (RX).

| Board | Turns | Track / gap (mm) | Track length | DC resistance, 2 oz on all layers | with 1 oz inner layers | Inductance in air |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `idra_coil_TX20` | 19.9 | 0.755 / 0.25 | 1.82 m | 0.30 Ω | 0.39 Ω | about 15 µH |
| `idra_coil_RX24` | 23.9 | 0.600 / 0.25 | 2.19 m | 0.45 Ω | 0.60 Ω | about 21 µH |
| `idra_coil_RX28` | 27.9 | 0.487 / 0.25 | 2.56 m | 0.65 Ω | 0.86 Ω | about 28 µH |

All figures are calculated, not measured. Resistance is at 20 °C and at DC: at 100–200 kHz it is higher (skin and proximity effect in wide flat tracks), by an amount that has not been calculated. Inductance is the textbook formula for a flat spiral in air; the ferrite sheet behind both coils raises it by an amount that has to be measured.

The turn counts are starting values (`mechanical/cad/WRIST_BUILD.md`). `RX28` is there in case `RX24` gives less than 9 V in the hand under load.

## The board
- **Outline:** ring, Ø43.6 outside, Ø15.6 inside, 1.6 mm thick.
- **Layers:** 4. Layers 1 and 2 are in parallel and spiral inwards from terminal A; layers 3 and 4 are in parallel and spiral back outwards, in the same sense, to terminal B. Three vias join the four layers at the inner end.
- **Terminals:** two solder pads 3.0 × 1.2 mm on the rim, 40° apart, present on both faces, each with three vias.
- **Vias:** 0.3 mm drill, 0.6 mm pad, covered by solder mask.
- **Label:** `TX20`, `RX24` or `RX28` on the rim opposite the terminals, on both faces.

## Ordering
- 4 layers, FR-4, 1.6 mm, **2 oz (70 µm) copper on outer and inner layers** if the fab offers it. With 1 oz inner layers the resistance is a third higher (table).
- Minimum track and gap here are 0.49 and 0.25 mm; minimum drill 0.3 mm.
- Any surface finish. No assembly, no stencil.
- Order `TX20` and `RX24`; add `RX28` as a spare option.

## In the pocket
The pocket in the CAD (wrist v1.29 and later) is 2.4 mm deep: two rings of 0.3 mm flexible ferrite sheet on the floor (Ø44 / Ø15, notched under the pads), 0.2 mm of adhesive, then the board with its copper face flush with the rim, facing the other coil across the 1 mm gap. Solder the two leads to the back of the pads and pass them through the two holes under them.

## What was checked
- The Gerber and drill files load in an independent reader (gerbonara) as a complete 4-layer stack with outline and 9 plated holes, and the rendering matches the intended geometry.
- The KiCad file parses (kiutils). **It has not been opened in KiCad itself**: open it and run the design rule check before ordering.
- Geometry: 0.25 mm between turns, 0.9 mm or more between each terminal and the winding of the other half, 0.35 mm from pads to the board edge.
- Everything is one net (`COIL`) in the KiCad file, because the coil is one piece of copper from A to B.
