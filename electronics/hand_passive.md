# Passive hand: electronics (hand v2.3, wrist v1.23)

The hand has no microcontroller, no radio and no firmware. The command is the frequency of the inductive carrier, chosen by the camera XIAO on the wrist flange:

| Carrier | The hand |
|---|---|
| ≈ 98 kHz | closes |
| ≈ 207 kHz | opens |
| off | stays still: the worm holds the grip |

Schematics: `rx_hand.svg` (hand) and `tx_flange.svg` (flange), generated by `hand_passive_schematic.py`. The page `hand_passive.html` shows them together with these values.

## Hand (RX)

**Power:**
- RX coil, 4 × SS24 bridge rectifier, VM 10–15 V with 47 µF 50 V, SMBJ30A TVS and 1 µF;
- a DRV8871 drives the 25 mm gear motor (12 V, 75:1);
- R_ILIM 82 kΩ sets the current limit: about 64/R[kΩ], so about 0.78 A (check on the datasheet). The current limit sets the grip force: see `control/hand_grip_report.md`.

**Detectors:** they take their signal from the top coil terminal, which swings between 0 and VM. Each one is:
- a filter: 2.2 mH inductor, C0G capacitor and resistor R to ground;
- a 1N4148W diode followed by 4.7 nF ‖ 10 kΩ (τ = 47 µs);
- a 3.3 V zener in series, then 47 kΩ to ground and a 5.6 V zener protecting the input.

| Channel | Filter | Frequency | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1.2 nF + 470 Ω | 98 kHz | IN1 (close) |
| B | 270 pF + 1 kΩ, with the NC limit switch in series | 207 kHz | IN2 (open) |

**Design numbers:**
- **Selectivity:** Q ≈ 2.9 on both channels. The other frequency gets through at about 21%, under 2 V after the diode, and the zener blocks it.
- **Right channel:** the fundamental is about 0.64·VM. With VM = 10 V, IN reaches about 2.5 V; it needs more than 1.5 V.
- **Both inputs high:** the DRV brakes, so the worst mistake is harmless.

## Flange (TX)

**Bridge:**
- IR2104, powered at 12 V by a 78L12 from the 24 V;
- US1D + 1 µF bootstrap;
- 2 N-MOSFETs: at least 40 V, R_DS(on) ≤ 50 mΩ, Q_g ≤ 20 nC;
- 10 Ω gate resistors.

**Coil:** from the SW node through a 1 µF 100 V film capacitor, then the TX coil, then local ground. The bridge is not tuned: both frequencies pass and the MOSFETs switch at zero voltage.

**Current sense:** local ground goes to ground through a 0.1 Ω shunt. An INA180A2 (×50), followed by a 1 kΩ / 100 nF filter, brings the signal to the XIAO ADC.

**XIAO connections:** 50% PWM on IN, enable on SD, current on the ADC. The cable to the camera goes from 2 to 5 wires.

**Warning:** do not use self-oscillating ZVS modules.

## XIAO firmware

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| open() | 207 kHz until the current drops to the idle level (limit switch): this is the finger zero |
| close() | 98 kHz until the current stays above the stall threshold for 30 ms, then the bridge turns off |
| open_to(θ) | open(), then close for a calibrated time |
| speed | 2–5 kHz bursts with variable duty |
| hold | bridge off |

Every move has a 4 s timeout. The emergency button cuts the 24 V.

## Worm drive

With module 2, 16 mm pitch diameter and a single start, the lead angle is λ = 7.1°. The worm is self-locking when atan(µ/cos 20°) is larger than λ, that is for µ above about 0.12.

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Dry PETG on PETG (µ 0.2–0.3) | holds with margin |
| Greased PETG (µ about 0.08) | back-drives |

At most a film of dry PTFE. Forward efficiency is about 36%.

## Bench test (before the final CAD)

1. Measure the inductance of the PCB coils (`coil_pcb/`) at the real 1 mm gap, with the ferrite sheet behind them.
2. Run the TX alone at both frequencies: the MOSFETs stay warm at most, and the rectified open-circuit voltage stays below 30 V.
3. With a 15 Ω, 10 W load (the stall current of the hand), VM stays between 10 and 15 V. Below 9 V, use the `RX28` coil board (more turns).
4. Sweep the frequency while watching IN1 and IN2: the right channel goes above 2 V and the wrong one stays at 0. Note the peak frequencies.
5. Connect the DRV and motor: the direction follows the frequency, and the limit switch stops the opening.
6. Log the TX-side current and derive the stall and idle thresholds.
7. Close on an object, turn the bridge off and shake the hand: the fingers must not back off.
8. Run 20 cycles: everything stays below 50 °C, the coil boards below 60 °C.
